Privacy Policy

Calc Plus · Wuxi Origin Technology Co., Ltd.

English 中文

Last updated: August 2026

Wuxi Origin Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") operates Calc Plus. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

The app does not require account registration or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or other contact information through the app.

2. Local Storage on Your Device

Calculation history, custom templates, language preferences, and other settings are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers unless you choose to share it outside the app. You can remove it by deleting the app or clearing app data.

3. Network and Third-Party Services

When you use the app, we and our service providers may collect certain information through third-party SDKs, including:

  • Firebase Analytics: app usage events (such as opening a calculator or using a template), device model, operating system version, app version, language, and a per-session identifier. We do not collect the numbers you enter or your calculation results.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs, stack traces, and related device information to help us diagnose and fix stability issues.
  • Google AdMob: advertising identifiers and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Ad serving requires a network connection.

These services are operated by Google LLC and are governed by their privacy policies. See https://policies.google.com/privacy for more information.

4. Core Features and Network Access

Core calculator features can be used without a network connection. Analytics, crash reporting, ads, and remote configuration require network access.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version within the app and on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Wuxi Origin Technology Co., Ltd. through the app store listing or official support channels.

无锡起源科技有限公司（以下简称「我们」）运营 Calc Plus。本隐私政策说明您使用本应用时我们如何处理相关信息。

1. 我们不直接收集的信息

本应用无需注册或登录，不会通过应用向您索取姓名、手机号、电子邮箱等联系方式。

2. 设备本地存储

计算历史、自定义模板、语言偏好及其他设置均保存在您的设备本地。除非您主动在应用外分享，否则这些数据不会上传至我们的服务器。您可通过卸载应用或清除应用数据将其删除。

3. 网络与第三方服务

您使用本应用时，我们及第三方服务提供商可能通过 SDK 收集以下信息：

  • Firebase Analytics（分析）：应用使用事件（如打开计算器、使用模板）、设备型号、操作系统版本、应用版本、语言及每次启动的会话标识。我们不会收集您输入的数值或计算结果。
  • Firebase Crashlytics（崩溃上报）：崩溃日志、堆栈信息及相关设备信息，用于诊断和修复稳定性问题。
  • Google AdMob（广告）：广告标识符及广告互动数据，用于投放和衡量广告。广告服务需要网络连接。

上述服务由 Google LLC 运营，受其隐私政策约束。详见 https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. 核心功能与网络访问

核心计算功能可在无网络环境下使用。分析、崩溃上报、广告及远程配置需要网络访问。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 政策更新

我们可能不时修订本隐私政策。重大变更将在应用内更新版本及本页面中体现。

7. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请通过应用商店页面或官方支持渠道联系无锡起源科技有限公司。
